ZAGO Passenger Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ZAGO (“ZAGO,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you use the ZAGO Passenger mobile application and related ride-booking services. It applies to passengers using the app. The information actually collected can depend on the features you use and the permissions you grant.

1. Information we may collect

  • Account and contact information: your phone number for registration, sign-in, and account verification, and profile details you choose to provide.
  • Location and trip information: pickup and destination locations, coordinates, and location information used by ride-booking features when you grant the relevant device permission. The app may also use a device's last-known location or refresh location to help set a pickup point.
  • Ride and transaction-related details: ride requests, fare offers and counters, matched rider information, booking status, ride verification/OTP status, trip timestamps, and ride history. This description does not mean ZAGO necessarily processes card or bank payments.
  • Device and diagnostic information: technical data such as app/device details, logs, and diagnostic information that may be processed by the app or its service providers to keep the service working, secure it, and troubleshoot problems.
  • Support communications: information you include if you contact us for help or make a privacy request.

2. How we use information

We use information as reasonably necessary to create and authenticate accounts, set pickup and destination points, find and connect passengers with riders, support fare negotiation and ride verification, display ride status and history, respond to support requests, prevent fraud or misuse, maintain and secure the service, and meet applicable legal obligations.

3. Location permissions

Location is used to support pickup selection, nearby rider matching, and trip-related features. You can manage location permissions through your device settings. If you deny or disable permission, you may need to enter a location manually and some features may not work as intended. Location use depends on the permissions granted and the feature being used. We will update this policy if the app's location practices materially change.

4. How information is shared

  • Matched riders: information needed to arrange and complete a ride may be shared with the rider assigned to or responding to your request. This may include pickup/destination details, relevant trip status, and limited passenger or contact details needed for the ride.
  • Service providers: the service relies on third-party infrastructure and services for functions such as phone authentication, maps/location, hosting, databases, and technical operations. Those providers process information as needed to provide their services under their own terms and privacy practices.
  • Legal, safety, and business needs: information may be disclosed when required by law, to respond to valid legal requests, to protect users and the service, or as part of a legitimate business transfer where permitted by law.

We do not sell personal information for money. We do not share information for purposes unrelated to operating or protecting the service except as described in this policy or where you direct us or the law permits it.

5. Third-party services

Some features depend on third-party services, which may process information under their own privacy policies. Examples include Firebase/Google services for phone authentication and Google Maps Platform services for maps or location-related features, where enabled. Please review the relevant provider policies for more information. We do not control the independent privacy practices of third-party providers.

6. Data retention

We keep information for as long as reasonably needed to provide the service, maintain ride and account records, address disputes and safety issues, meet legal requirements, and enforce our agreements. Retention periods vary by data type and purpose. Information may be deleted or de-identified when it is no longer needed, subject to lawful retention requirements.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information. No app, network, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure, and we cannot promise absolute security.

8. Your choices and privacy requests

You can manage device permissions in your phone settings. To request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information associated with your account, email support.zago@gmail.com from your registered email address if available, and include your registered phone number and the request details. Do not email passwords or one-time verification codes. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

We will review and respond to requests as required by applicable law. Some information may need to be retained for legitimate safety, dispute-resolution, fraud-prevention, or legal reasons. Contacting us does not guarantee that every record can be deleted immediately.

9. Children's privacy

ZAGO Passenger is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 18. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children under 18. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us so we can review the matter.

10. International processing

Our service providers may process or store information in locations outside your state or country. Where applicable, we take steps required by law for such processing.

11. Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy as the app, our practices, or legal requirements change. We will post the updated version on this page and update the effective date. Please review it periodically.
