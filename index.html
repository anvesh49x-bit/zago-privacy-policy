ZAGO Passenger Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

ZAGO provides a motorcycle ride-booking service that connects passengers with riders. This policy explains how the ZAGO Passenger app handles personal information.

Information We Collect

  • Phone number used for account registration and verification.
  • Location information used to set pickup and drop-off points, find nearby riders, and support trips.
  • Ride details, including pickup, destination, fare offers, booking status, and trip history.
  • Profile information you provide and technical information needed to operate, protect, and troubleshoot the service.

How We Use Information

We use information to create and secure accounts, arrange rides, connect passengers with riders, support navigation and trip status, prevent misuse, and maintain the service.

Sharing of Information

Information necessary to arrange a ride may be shared with the matched rider. We may use service providers for authentication, hosting, maps, and technical operations. We do not sell personal information.

Location

Location access helps provide pickup, destination, and ride matching features. You can manage location permissions in your device settings. Some features may not work without location access.

Data Security and Retention

We use reasonable measures to protect information and retain it only as needed for service delivery, legitimate business purposes, and applicable legal requirements.

Your Choices and Requests

You may manage device permissions and contact ZAGO to ask about accessing, correcting, or deleting your personal information, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Children's Privacy

ZAGO Passenger is not intended for children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when our service or data practices change. Updates will be published on this page.
