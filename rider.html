ZAGO Rider Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ZAGO (“ZAGO,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you use the ZAGO Rider mobile application and related ride services. It applies to people registering or working as riders/captains on ZAGO. The information processed depends on the features you use, the information you submit, and the permissions you grant.

1. Information we may collect

  • Account and contact information: phone number used for registration, sign-in, and verification, plus profile details you provide.
  • Identity and onboarding documents: information and images from documents you submit to apply to become a ZAGO rider. Depending on the onboarding requirements, these may include a driving licence, identity document, vehicle registration certificate, vehicle insurance, permit, or other documents requested for eligibility and verification. Only submit documents requested in the app or by an authorized ZAGO representative.
  • Profile photos and camera images: a profile photo or other image you choose or are asked to submit for onboarding or verification, where that feature is available.
  • Location information: current or last-known location and location updates used for availability, nearby ride requests, pickup navigation, and active-trip features. The app may request foreground or background location permission for location-dependent features.
  • Ride and service information: availability status, ride requests, fare offers and counteroffers, acceptance or rejection, trip status, verification/OTP status, timestamps, and ride history.
  • Device and diagnostic information: technical information, logs, and diagnostic details processed to operate, maintain, secure, and troubleshoot the app.
  • Support communications: details you include when contacting us about your account, onboarding, documents, a trip, or a privacy request.

2. How we use information

We use information to create and authenticate rider accounts; review onboarding applications and submitted documents; assess eligibility to use rider features; help prevent fraud, impersonation, and misuse; show and match ride requests; support fare negotiation, trip coordination, and ride verification; provide location-dependent features; maintain app security and reliability; respond to support requests; and meet applicable legal obligations.

3. Document review and verification

Documents you submit are used for rider onboarding, eligibility checks, and related safety, trust, and compliance reviews. Authorized personnel responsible for rider onboarding or account support may access them for these purposes. We do not intend for your submitted onboarding documents to be displayed publicly to passengers. Where third-party hosting, storage, authentication, or technical service providers process data on our behalf, they may process it as needed to provide those services.

Please make sure documents and details you submit are accurate and belong to you. Do not submit another person's documents unless you are legally authorized to do so.

4. Location and camera permissions

Location may be used to show availability, deliver nearby ride requests, coordinate pickup, and support active trips. Some features may require location access while the app is in use; background location may be requested where needed for an enabled ride or availability feature. You can manage permissions in your device settings, but denying them may prevent certain rider features from working.

Camera access is used when you choose or are asked to capture a profile photo or onboarding/verification image. You can manage camera permission in your device settings. The app's actual behavior depends on the feature you use and the permissions you grant.

5. How information is shared

  • Passengers and ride participants: information needed to arrange and complete a ride may be shared, such as rider display/profile details, relevant trip status, and location or arrival information. Onboarding documents such as a driving licence are not intended to be shared with passengers as part of ordinary ride matching.
  • Authorized ZAGO personnel: relevant information and onboarding documents may be accessed by people responsible for onboarding review, safety, support, and service operation.
  • Service providers: providers supporting authentication, maps/location, hosting, databases, notifications, storage, or technical operations may process information as necessary to provide their services.
  • Legal and safety purposes: information may be disclosed where required by applicable law or valid legal process, or where reasonably necessary to investigate fraud, protect users, or protect the service.

We do not sell personal information for money. We do not share onboarding documents with unrelated third parties for their own marketing.

6. Third-party services

The app may rely on third-party services, including Firebase/Google services for phone authentication, Google Maps Platform for maps or location-related features, and the hosting, database, or storage services used by ZAGO. These providers may process information under their own terms and privacy practices. The providers actually used may change as the service evolves.

7. Data retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for account operation, onboarding and verification, ride services, safety and fraud prevention, dispute handling, and legal obligations. Document retention may depend on the status of your application or account and applicable requirements. Retention periods can vary by data type. We will delete or de-identify information when it is no longer reasonably needed, subject to lawful retention requirements.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure. No online service or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Your choices, access, and deletion requests

You can manage device permissions through your phone settings. To request access to, correction of, or deletion of your account information or onboarding documents, email support.zago@gmail.com. Include your registered phone number and explain your request. Do not email passwords or one-time verification codes. We may ask for information to verify your identity before acting on a request.

We will review and respond as required by applicable law. Some records may need to be retained for legal, safety, fraud-prevention, or dispute-resolution purposes. Sending a request does not guarantee that every record can be deleted immediately. If you want to stop using ZAGO, you can also contact us to request account deletion.

10. Children's privacy

ZAGO Rider is intended for adults who meet the applicable eligibility requirements to provide rides. It is not directed to children under 18. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children under 18.

11. International processing

Our service providers may process or store information outside your state or country. Where applicable, we take steps required by law for such processing.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app, our practices, or legal requirements change. The updated policy will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. Please review it periodically.
